# Foodie

An Android app for discovering Michelin Guide restaurants near you, or anywhere you're travelling, and keeping track of the ones you want to try or have visited.

## Features
- Find restaurants within 30 km of your GPS location.
- Search any city or place.
- List and map views for browsing results.
- Filters for stars and Bib Gourmand, plus a cuisine picker.
- Restaurant details: award, cuisine, price, address, description, facilities and services, opening hours and a photo, with one-tap call and directions
- Favourites and Visited lists, with notes on places you've been.
- Backup and restore of your favourites and visited places to a JSON file.
- Works offline once the catalogue is downloaded.
